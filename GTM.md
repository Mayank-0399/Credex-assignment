# Go-To-Market Strategy

## Target Users

The primary users for AI Spend Audit are:

* small startups
* solo developers
* AI-heavy engineering teams
* creators using multiple AI subscriptions

These users often subscribe to several AI tools but do not actively track whether the pricing plans are actually worth the cost.

---

## Problem Being Solved

Many teams end up:

* paying for overlapping AI tools
* using expensive plans unnecessarily
* losing track of monthly AI spending

There is currently no simple tool focused specifically on helping smaller teams understand and optimize AI software costs.

---

## Product Positioning

AI Spend Audit is positioned as:

* a lightweight AI cost optimization tool
* simple to use
* fast to generate reports
* focused on actionable savings recommendations

Instead of complex enterprise analytics dashboards, the goal is to provide quick and understandable insights.

---

## Distribution Strategy

### Initial Channels

* LinkedIn posts
* developer communities
* Reddit communities
* startup founder groups
* portfolio/project showcases

---

## Why Shareable Reports Matter

The shareable audit URLs help users:

* send reports to teammates
* discuss optimization decisions
* review spending together

This also helps organic product sharing and visibility.

---

## Monetization Ideas

Possible future monetization:

* premium organization dashboards
* advanced analytics
* historical spending tracking
* enterprise reporting
* procurement consulting

---

## Future Expansion

Possible future improvements:

* API usage analysis
* real AI-powered recommendations
* multi-user collaboration
* SaaS spend tracking beyond AI tools
* company-wide cost monitoring

---

## Success Metrics

Some useful product metrics would be:

* number of completed audits
* average estimated savings
* report share rate
* returning users
* lead conversion rate
