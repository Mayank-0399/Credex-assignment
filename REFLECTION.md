# Reflection

This project helped me understand how real full-stack SaaS products are structured beyond just frontend development. I worked across frontend UI, backend APIs, database integration, testing, deployment, and CI/CD workflows.

One of the biggest learnings was understanding the importance of architecture decisions early in development. Structuring the project properly made later features much easier to add.

I also learned that product thinking matters as much as coding. Features like shareable audit URLs, personalized summaries, and lead capture improved the project significantly because they made the app feel like a usable product instead of only a technical demo.

The most challenging part was balancing simplicity with realism. I wanted the audit recommendations to feel believable without relying entirely on expensive AI APIs, so I chose deterministic audit logic that remains predictable and explainable.

Overall, this project improved my confidence in building and deploying complete full-stack applications independently.
