# Metrics

## Product Metrics

The following metrics would help evaluate product performance and user engagement.

---

## Core Usage Metrics

### Total Audits Generated

Tracks how many audit reports users create.

Why it matters:

* indicates overall product usage
* measures adoption growth

---

### Average Estimated Savings

Measures the average savings identified per audit.

Why it matters:

* shows perceived product value
* helps validate recommendation quality

---

### Share Rate

Tracks how often users share audit URLs.

Why it matters:

* indicates whether reports are valuable enough to share
* supports organic product growth

---

### Lead Conversion Rate

Measures how many users save reports after generating audits.

Why it matters:

* reflects engagement quality
* useful for future monetization

---

## Technical Metrics

### API Response Time

Tracks summary generation and lead API performance.

Goal:

* maintain fast and smooth user experience

---

### Deployment Stability

Tracks:

* failed builds
* CI/CD failures
* production deployment reliability

---

## Future Metrics

Potential future metrics:

* returning users
* organization retention
* audit completion rate
* team collaboration activity
* premium conversion rate
